## Starting point

Status: confirmed · Source: person

> The Steward: protects what matters over the long term.

## Who it is for

Status: confirmed · Source: person

> Volunteer coordinators at small charities who run sign-ups on spreadsheets
> and goodwill.

## Where they meet the work

Status: confirmed · Source: person

> On a laptop after the day job, often the night before an event, when a
> sign-up sheet has broken again.

## The change to help bring about

Status: confirmed · Source: person

> Coordinators spend their evenings with volunteers, not fixing forms.

## What it stands for

Status: confirmed · Source: person

> Plain words. Forms that work on an old phone. Data that belongs to the
> charity.

## What it stands against

Status: confirmed · Source: person

> Forms that push people to share more than they need to. Pricing that
> punishes small teams for growing.

## How it works differently

Status: confirmed · Source: guidelines

> Every form works without JavaScript, and the person who fills it in never
> needs an account.

## What it will not do

Status: confirmed · Source: person

> We will not sell or share sign-up data. We will not add tracking to forms.

## What people should experience

Status: suggestion · Source: agent-inferred

> Relief: the form just works, and nobody has to ask for help.
